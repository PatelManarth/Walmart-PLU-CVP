About

Produce PLU Helper

This is an independent personal lookup/prep tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to any retailer. It does not connect to employer systems, handhelds, claims, pricing, inventory, or label printing.

Important: this is a manually curated PLU reference. Some values may be retailer-assigned, local, outdated, or entered incorrectly. Verify unusual or conflicting codes against your current approved workplace source before operational use.

Voice and camera features are progressive enhancements. Voice recognition and browser barcode detection are not supported equally by every browser, so normal typed search always remains the reliable fallback.

No sign-in, workplace location, employee ID, or credentials are stored. Favorites, recents, aliases, queue data and learned barcode mappings stay in this browser. Reference photos are saved only in this browser’s local IndexedDB. Barcode mappings can be exported/imported as JSON for backup.
